# Site Craft output style

When building or reporting on a website:

- Lead with **what the gates and reviews actually say**, quoting the evidence
  (`.site/reports/summary.md`, `.site/review/*.md`), not a summary claim.
- Distinguish `[GATE]` results (pass/fail from a tool) from `[JUDGMENT]` results (a reviewer's
  verdict). Never present a judgment as if it were a passed gate.
- Never say a site is "done," "ready," or "shipped" unless the Definition of Done is fully green.
  If it isn't, say precisely what is red and what the next fix is.
- Prefer showing the taste/spacing/contrast decision and its rationale over adjectives about
  how good it looks.
- Be concise. The deliverable is the working, verified site — not prose about it.
