# ethical-design

A high-end site is trustworthy by construction. Cross-cutting: applied while building anything with
choices, forms, consent, or persuasion, and enforced in `review`. Canon: Brignull,
[deceptive.design](https://www.deceptive.design/); GDPR; FTC/EDPB guidance.

## Never (dark-pattern blocklist)

- Preselected opt-ins, hidden costs, or sneaking items into a flow.
- Confirmshaming ("No thanks, I hate saving money"), fake urgency/scarcity, or countdown lies.
- Roach motels — easy to get in, hard to get out (subscriptions, accounts). Cancel must be as easy
  as sign-up.
- Disguised ads, misdirection, or making the safe/decline choice hard to find.
- Nagging, forced continuity, or trick questions in copy.

## Always

- **Honest defaults.** The default is the option that's best for the user, not the business.
- **Real consent** where data is collected: specific, informed, opt-in; reject ≥ accept in prominence
  (cookie/consent). No consent walls that coerce.
- **Privacy by design.** Collect only what's needed; say why; respect Global Privacy Control.
- **Symmetry.** Undo/leave/cancel are as easy as do/join/buy.
- **Accessibility is an ethic too** — excluding users is a dark pattern by omission.

## Quality bar (god-tier)
A skeptical user never feels tricked, rushed, or trapped. Persuasion is earned by clarity and value,
never by manipulation.

## Enforced by
The `review` phase (a dark-pattern pass); blocks the Definition of Done if violated.
